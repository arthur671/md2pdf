## **§ 1 Scope of Work**

| Deliverable | Description | Due date |
|---|---|---|
| Design concept | Two layout options, one revision round | 20 March 2026 |
| Website build | Up to 8 pages, responsive, CMS based | 30 April 2026 |
| Handover | Training session & documentation | 8 May 2026 |

## **§ 2 Fees and Payment**

- **Fixed fee:** 6,400.00 EUR (net)
- **Additional work:** 85.00 EUR per hour, only after written approval
- **Payment terms:** 14 days after invoice, to the account given on the invoice

The fee is paid in three instalments:

1. 30 % on signature of this agreement
2. 40 % on approval of the design concept
3. 30 % on handover of the finished website

## **§ 3 Rights and Confidentiality**

On full payment, all usage rights to the delivered designs pass to the Client. Both parties keep all information about the other party confidential, also after the end of this agreement. Questions: <https://example.com/contact>

The agreement ends with the handover in § 1. Either party may terminate it for good cause in writing. Work completed up to that date is paid pro rata.

- **Changes:** Only valid in writing
- **Severability:** If one clause is invalid, the rest of the agreement stays in force
- **Copies:** Each party receives one signed copy
